# Corporate Governance & Compliance Study: Shopify Inc.

## Executive Summary
This compliance study analyzes **Shopify Inc.** across its primary regulatory frameworks, data privacy mandates, and industry security standards. As an enterprise SaaS platform processing billions in Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV), Shopify operates under a shared responsibility model where it acts as a **Data Processor** for merchants while maintaining compliance as a **Data Controller** for platform-level analytics and operations.

---

## 1. Compliance Framework Mapping

| Regulatory Framework | Applicability Domain | Mandatory Requirements | Shopify Compliance Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PCI DSS v4.0** | Payment Systems & Merchant Solutions | Level 1 Service Provider compliance, annual Attestation of Compliance (AoC), quarterly ASV vulnerability scans. | Certified PCI DSS Level 1 compliant; annual AoC and quarterly ASV attestation reports provided. |
| **SOC 1 / SOC 2 / SOC 3** | Cloud Security & Operational Integrity | AICPA Trust Services Criteria (Security, Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity). | Annual SOC 1 Type 2, SOC 2 Type 2 audits, and public SOC 3 reports issued with bridge letters. |
| **GDPR / UK GDPR** | Data Privacy (EEA & UK Citizens) | Lawful basis for processing, Data Processing Addendums (DPAs), Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs), cross-border transfers. | Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), built-in merchant privacy controls, DPA alignment. |
| **CCPA / CPRA** | Data Privacy (California Residents) | Consumer opt-out mechanisms ("Do Not Sell My Info"), data minimization, deletion workflows. | Privacy control toggles, Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal integration across checkout platforms. |
| **SEC / SOX (Sec. 404)** | Public Financial & Operational Controls | Internal Controls Over Financial Reporting (ICFR), financial audits, public disclosures (10-K / 40-F). | Annual management assessments and independent auditor attestations on internal financial controls. |
